Behind the camera since 2011.

Fifteen-plus years of documentary and commercial work sit behind every still on this page. What started with a Nikon D3100 in 2011 has grown into a full production practice — but the eye behind the lens hasn't changed.

This is the stills side of that same #ShotbyaHuman approach: real people, real light, real moments — not generated, not staged into something unrecognizable.
